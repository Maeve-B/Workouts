Chest

Bench + Dumbbells
Flat Bench Press
20reps
10–12kg each dumbbell (estimate — adjust after set 1)
Bottom
Lockout
  1. Lie back on the bench, feet flat on the floor, dumbbells held above your chest with arms extended.
  2. Lower the weights slowly, elbows bending out to about 45° from your body, until upper arms are roughly level with the bench.
  3. Press back up in a straight line until arms are extended, without locking the elbows hard.
  4. Keep shoulder blades pulled back and down into the bench throughout.
Cue: Drive through your palms, not your fingers. Exhale on the press up.
Bench Flyes
16reps
5–6kg each dumbbell
Wide (stretch)
Top (squeeze)
  1. Lie on the bench holding dumbbells above your chest, palms facing each other, a soft bend in the elbows.
  2. Lower the weights out to the sides in a wide arc, keeping the elbow bend fixed, until you feel a stretch across the chest.
  3. Bring the dumbbells back up in the same arc, as if hugging a large barrel, squeezing the chest at the top.
Cue: Elbow angle stays constant — this is a hinge at the shoulder, not a press.
Push-Ups
15reps
Bodyweight — no added load
Top
Bottom
  1. Hands on the bench (or floor for more challenge) slightly wider than shoulders, body in a straight line from head to heels.
  2. Lower your chest toward your hands, elbows tracking back at roughly 45°.
  3. Push back up to full extension without letting your hips sag or pike up.
Cue: Brace your core like someone's about to poke your stomach — keeps the line straight.

Triceps

Dumbbells + Bench
Lying Tricep Extensions
16reps
3–4kg each dumbbell
Start (arms up)
Bend (bar to forehead)
  1. Lie on the bench holding dumbbells (or a barbell) straight above your shoulders, arms extended.
  2. Keeping your upper arms still and pointing at the ceiling, bend only at the elbow to lower the weight toward your forehead.
  3. Extend back up by straightening the elbows, without moving the shoulder joint.
Cue: Elbows stay narrow and fixed in space — think "hinge," not "swing."
Close-Grip Bench Press
16reps
15–18kg barbell (general estimate)
Bottom
Lockout
  1. Grip the barbell with hands about shoulder-width apart (narrower than a normal bench press).
  2. Lower the bar to your lower chest, elbows tucked in close to your body rather than flared out.
  3. Press back up, focusing on straightening the elbows through the top of the movement.
Cue: Keep elbows brushing your sides on the way down to keep tension on triceps, not shoulders.
Tricep Kickbacks
16reps
3–4kg each dumbbell
Elbow bent 90°
Extended back
  1. Hinge forward from the hips, flat back, one hand supported on the bench, dumbbell in the other hand with elbow bent at 90° and tucked at your side.
  2. Keeping your upper arm still, straighten the elbow to extend the dumbbell back until your arm is in line with your torso.
  3. Pause briefly, then bend the elbow to return to the start.
Cue: Only the forearm moves. If your elbow drifts, the weight's too heavy.

Biceps

Barbell + Dumbbells
Barbell Curl
20reps
12–15kg barbell (general estimate)
Arms extended
Curled up
  1. Stand tall, grip the barbell shoulder-width apart, arms extended, elbows close to your sides.
  2. Curl the bar up toward your shoulders by bending the elbows, keeping upper arms pinned to your sides.
  3. Squeeze at the top, then lower with control back to full extension.
Cue: No swinging — if your hips are helping, drop the weight.
Alternating Dumbbell Curl
20reps
6–7kg each dumbbell
One arm down
Other arm up
  1. Stand holding a dumbbell in each hand, arms extended, palms facing forward (or neutral to start).
  2. Curl one dumbbell up while rotating your palm to face your shoulder at the top; the other arm stays extended.
  3. Lower that arm as you curl the opposite side, alternating each rep.
Cue: Keep the resting arm fully extended and relaxed — don't let it creep up.
Hammer Curl
16reps
6–7kg each dumbbell
Down (thumbs up)
Curled (neutral grip)
  1. Hold dumbbells with palms facing each other (neutral grip), arms extended by your sides.
  2. Curl both dumbbells up simultaneously, keeping the neutral grip throughout — thumbs stay pointing up.
  3. Squeeze at the top, then lower under control.
Cue: This grip biases the forearm and outer bicep — keep wrists straight, not cocked.

Shoulders

Dumbbells + Bench
Overhead Press
16reps
14–15kg — scaled from your 5x5 (20kg)
Racked at shoulders
Pressed overhead
  1. Stand with feet hip-width, dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward, core braced.
  2. Press the weights straight up until arms are fully extended overhead, letting them travel slightly inward at the top.
  3. Lower back to shoulder height with control.
Cue: Avoid arching your lower back — brace your core to keep ribs stacked over hips.
Lateral Raises
16reps
3–4kg each dumbbell
Arms down
Arms level with shoulders
  1. Stand holding dumbbells by your sides, a slight bend in the elbows, palms facing your body.
  2. Raise both arms out to the sides until they reach shoulder height, leading with the elbows.
  3. Lower back down with control — don't let momentum carry the weight.
Cue: Imagine pouring water from a jug as you raise — slight tilt, don't shrug your traps up.
Upright Row
16reps
12–15kg barbell (general estimate)
Bar at hips
Bar to chin height
  1. Hold the barbell with an overhand grip, hands about shoulder-width apart, resting against your thighs.
  2. Pull the bar straight up along your body, leading with your elbows, until it reaches roughly chin/collarbone height.
  3. Lower back down with control.
Cue: Elbows stay higher than your wrists throughout the pull.

Core

Bench + Light Plate
Plank
45sec
Bodyweight — no added load
Straight line
Avoid: hips sagging
  1. Forearms on the bench (or floor), elbows under shoulders, body extended in a straight line to your heels.
  2. Brace your abs and squeeze your glutes to keep hips level — not piked up, not sagging down.
  3. Breathe steadily and hold the position for the full time.
Cue: Tuck your chin slightly and look at the floor to keep your neck neutral.
Russian Twists
20reps
4–6kg plate
Twist left
Twist right
  1. Sit on the floor or bench edge, knees bent, leaning back slightly to about a 45° torso angle, holding a light plate or dumbbell with both hands.
  2. Feet can stay on the floor for an easier version, or lifted for more challenge.
  3. Rotate your torso to bring the weight to one side, then rotate through to the other side — that's one rep pair.
Cue: Rotate from your ribs, not just your arms — the weight should track with your torso.
Bicycle Crunches
20reps
Bodyweight — no added load
Right elbow to left knee
Left elbow to right knee
  1. Lie on your back, hands lightly behind your head, knees bent to 90°, lower back pressed into the floor.
  2. Bring one knee toward your chest while rotating to bring the opposite elbow toward it, extending the other leg out straight (not touching the floor).
  3. Switch sides in a smooth pedaling motion, alternating elbow to opposite knee.
Cue: Rotate from the ribs, and don't pull on your neck — hands just support the head's weight.
Cool-Down (3–5 min): Static stretching — quads, hamstrings, chest, lats, shoulders, triceps, biceps, hip flexors.
